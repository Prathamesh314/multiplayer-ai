Your team’s AI workspace.

Open your team’s workspace and sign in. This computer connects automatically to run your AI tasks.

1. Open your workspace

Use the HTTPS address supplied by your workspace administrator. This desktop app connects to a server; it does not host one.

This app opens . Sign in there with your account.

2. Your computer connects automatically

After you sign in, the app connects this computer and starts its runner. No pairing command is needed. Install Git and your preferred AI CLI, and sign in to that CLI.

The connection is reused when you reopen the app. You can pause it below; signing out stops new tasks after any accepted task finishes.

Only enable execution for a server you trust. Agents can run commands and access files with your user permissions. Provider requests may leave your computer.

Runner: not connected

3. Local worktrees

Each repository in a room has its own local worktree. Chats in that room share it, and the files stay on this computer between tasks.

Organized by repository, then room. Worktrees are created on first use. Pause the runner to change location; existing worktrees keep their saved paths.
