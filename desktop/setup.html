Your team’s AI workspace.
Connected to your computer.

Open your team’s workspace, then connect this computer to run AI tasks using your own tools.

1. Open your workspace

Use the HTTPS address supplied by your workspace administrator. This desktop app connects to a server; it does not host one.

2. Connect your AI tools

In your workspace, open Your computers → Connect a computer → Pair this desktop. Install Git and your preferred AI CLI first, and sign in to that CLI.

Only enable execution for a server you trust. Agents can run commands and access files with your user permissions. Provider requests may leave your computer.
